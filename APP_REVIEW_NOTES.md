# App Review Notes — Fitness Copilot

Fitness Copilot is a general fitness planning and exercise-technique guide. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or emergency guidance.
There is no user account or sign-in requirement. The reviewer can complete onboarding locally. Workout history is stored on-device.

## Review path

1. Launch the app and select a language.
2. Complete onboarding by choosing a goal, training location, available equipment, and level.
3. Browse exercises and open an exercise detail for written technique, safety guidance, and a video demonstration.
4. Start a workout from **My Plan** and record a set. Ads are suppressed while a workout, rest timer, or video is active.
5. Open **Muscle Atlas**. The advanced atlas is an optional Pro feature. The subscription uses Apple In-App Purchase through RevenueCat; no external purchase path is used.

## Subscription

The paywall loads localized prices from the current store offering. It shows an introductory trial only when the store reports eligibility, and explains automatic renewal and cancellation. Restore Purchases and subscription management are available. Apple presents final terms before purchase.
The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are linked from the paywall. Reviewers can dismiss the paywall and continue using free features. The 3D Muscle Atlas is a Pro feature; the exercise library and workout planning remain available without subscribing.

## Advertising

The free tier can display interstitial ads only during non-essential browsing breaks. Ads are never displayed during onboarding, an active workout, a rest timer, or exercise-video viewing. There are no banner ads.
The paywall contains a "Privacy and ad choices" control for reopening available AdMob privacy options.

## Video content

Direct MP4 demonstrations play inside the application. YouTube demonstrations on iOS and Android open in the operating system's in-app browser view, so the user remains in Fitness Copilot and the provider can apply its own playback and account policies.
The exercise detail also contains written movement steps and safety cues when a third-party video cannot play.
