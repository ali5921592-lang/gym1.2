# App Review Notes — Fitness Copilot

Fitness Copilot is a general fitness and exercise-technique guide for adults. It does not diagnose, treat, or provide emergency care. The app does not require an account or sign-in; onboarding, workout history, and preferences work on-device.

## Review path

1. Open the app and choose a language, or continue with the device’s preferred language.
2. Complete onboarding with any goal, location, equipment, and level.
3. Open **Exercises**, choose a movement, and review the written steps, form cues, safety notes, and available demonstration.
4. Open **My Plan**, review the four-week schedule, and start a session if desired. Ads are suppressed during an active workout, rest timer, and video playback.
5. Open **Muscle Atlas**. The free preview displays the interactive 3D anatomy model and allows rotation. Muscle selection, exercise matching, and the training heatmap are Pro subscription features. Once the Apple subscription products and RevenueCat entitlement are active, a StoreKit Sandbox purchase unlocks the full atlas; no reviewer account or password is required.

## Subscriptions

The intended monthly and annual auto-renewable products use Apple In-App Purchase through RevenueCat. After those products and introductory offers are configured in App Store Connect and RevenueCat, the app reads localized prices and trial eligibility from the store. A seven-day free offer is shown only when the store confirms eligibility. Apple allows each person to redeem one introductory offer per subscription group, even if both products have offers. The purchase sheet shows the applicable price and renewal terms before confirmation. Restore Purchases, subscription management, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Use are available in the app.

## Advertising

The free tier can display interstitial ads during eligible browsing breaks. There are no banner ads. Interstitials are suppressed during onboarding, workouts, rest timers, and exercise-video playback. The paywall includes the app’s available privacy and ad-choice controls.

## Video and anatomy

Exercise pages provide written instructions and safety cues as a fallback when a third-party demonstration cannot play. Videos are opened within the app’s browser view. The 3D atlas credits BodyParts3D, Z-Anatomy, and BodyExplorer in the app’s Sources screen and the bundled attribution file.
